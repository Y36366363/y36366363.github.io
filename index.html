Jialiang Yao

I am a master's student in Statistics, Analytics, and Data Science at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill . My interests lie at the intersection of statistical inference, AI-agent evaluation, simulation, and human-centered decision support.

My current work includes a faculty collaboration on poker decision support and human skill development, as well as independent research on statistical sensitivity analysis, strategic agents, and population simulation. I am particularly interested in research that is auditable, reproducible, and connected to clearly defined scientific questions.

I welcome conversations about research collaboration and doctoral study in statistics, computational social science, and human–AI decision-making.
